Nightscout Overlay

A small always-on-top glucose readout. Data goes straight from your Nightscout site to this app. Nothing else.
Connection
Needed if your site is not readable without logging in. In Nightscout: menu → Admin tools → Add new subject with role readable, then copy its token. Leave blank for public sites.
Display
Ranges (mg/dL)
Leave blank to use the thresholds from your Nightscout site.
Alerts
Tests use the current settings, including unsaved changes, and stay on the overlay until you press "I see it".
Alerts use the ranges above. An alert replays on this interval until the reading is back in range, whether or not you pressed "I see it" (that only hides the bar until the next replay). An alert that gets worse (low to urgent low) comes through straight away.
Behaviour
Shortcuts: Ctrl+Alt+G click-through, Ctrl+Alt+H hide/show, Ctrl+Alt+S settings. Right-click the overlay or the tray icon for the menu. Drag the overlay to move it. Drag its bottom-right corner, or Ctrl+scroll over it, to resize.
Updates
Installed builds download updates in the background and apply them when you restart or quit. Portable builds get a link to the new download instead.
