Nightscout Overlay

A small always-on-top glucose readout. Data goes straight from your Nightscout site to this app. Nothing else.
Connection
Needed if your site is not readable without logging in. In Nightscout: menu → Admin tools → Add new subject with role readable, then copy its token. Leave blank for public sites.
Display
Ranges (mg/dL)
Leave blank to use the thresholds from your Nightscout site.
Behaviour
Shortcuts: Ctrl+Alt+G click-through, Ctrl+Alt+H hide/show, Ctrl+Alt+S settings. Right-click the overlay or the tray icon for the menu. Drag the overlay to move it.
